04 Status Notes

Completed work

  • The onboarding and authentication flow (Screens 1–4) ran from 7 September and was delivered on 15 September 2026 — seven working days.
  • This covers Welcome / Intro, Loading, Register and Login — establishing the visual language, type scale, colour system and component patterns carried through every later phase.
  • No further work is scheduled on these screens unless changes are requested.

Upcoming milestones

  • 28 Sep 2026 — Phase 1, Core App Experience (7 screens). The next major milestone.
  • 2 Oct 2026 — Phase 2, User Account & Patient Management (7 screens), four working days after Phase 1.
  • 8 Oct 2026 — Phase 3, Additional Pages & Transaction Flow (6 screens, including the three-step booking flow, plus two supporting components), four working days after Phase 2.

Assumptions & dependencies

  • Phases run sequentially; each start date depends on the previous phase being delivered on schedule.
  • Durations count working days only — Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.
  • Content, branding assets and any required API or service details are assumed to be available at the start of each phase.
  • Screen counts and scope are as listed in Section 03. The schedule covers UI/UX design only; development and QA are outside this timeline.

05 Timeline Conditions

Review & feedback buffer

Each phase includes dedicated time for internal quality checks, client review, minor corrections and design refinements. These are built into the durations shown above rather than added on top of them.

Additional changes

Any major changes, additional screens, new requirements, or heavy design revisions requested after approval may require additional time and will be estimated separately based on the scope of changes.

Client dependency

Timeline may vary depending on the speed of feedback, approvals, and requirement clarifications from the client side.
